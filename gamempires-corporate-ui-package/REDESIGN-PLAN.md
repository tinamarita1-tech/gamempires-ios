# GamEmpires Chat: Corporate Workspace Redesign & Implementation Plan

## 1. Visual & UX Transformation Summary
- **Color Palette:** Replaced hazard-orange gaming gradients with neutral stone/slate surfaces and trustworthy Corporate Blue (#2563EB).
- **Geometry:** Converted angled sci-fi cutouts and hexagons into soft rounded-3xl cards and rounded-full pill controls.
- **Typography:** Migrated to Inter sans-serif with clean sentence casing for 8-hour workday readability.
- **Tone & Presence:** Replaced "// PLAYER ONE" and "CEO & HR ONLY" with warm greetings, interactive availability statuses, and collaborative announcements.

## 2. 4-Week Rollout Roadmap
- **Week 1 (Foundation):** Tokenize Light/Dark mode variables, load Inter font, standardize 24px stroke icons.
- **Week 2 (Navigation & Presence):** Build interactive availability picker, pill filter tabs, and unified search.
- **Week 3 (Workspace Widgets):** Wire Team Announcements, Google/M365 Calendar sync, and Message Reminders.
- **Week 4 (QA & Pilot):** WCAG AA contrast audit, 44px mobile tap-target check, and IT/HR beta rollout.
